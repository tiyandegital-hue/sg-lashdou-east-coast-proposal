Operational shift

An honest look at the transformation

Why Singapore boutique salons are upgrading from manual phone chats to intelligent WhatsApp booking automation.

Status quo

Lashdou today — manual WhatsApp

  • Slow replies during peak treatments — artists are performing delicate procedures and cannot answer their mobile.
  • Frustrating date ping-pong — many messages per customer just to agree on a slot, address details and pre-care notes.
  • Unrecovered late cancellations — when a client cancels hours ahead, staff are too occupied to text down the waitlist in time. The slot is lost.
  • Inconsistent retention — following up weeks later is easily forgotten when managing daily walk-ins.

The automated standard

Lashdou with the Tiyan concierge

  • Instant replies at any hour — polished service details and live openings, without disturbing artists mid-treatment.
  • Every slot locked quickly — direct calendar hold, instant WhatsApp buttons, calendar synchronisation and directions dispatched at once.
  • Automated waitlist recovery — sudden cancellations trigger an immediate ping to waitlisted clients, turning vacant chairs into revenue.
  • A compounding retention loop — automated aftercare and well-timed maintenance prompts safeguard lash health and bring clients back.
