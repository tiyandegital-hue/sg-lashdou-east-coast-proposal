Frequently considered questions

01Will it feel robotic to our clients?

Never. The conversational voice is specifically tuned to Lashdou's warm, polite East Coast boutique tone — warm porcelain and quiet precision, never generic corporate automated phrasing.

02Can staff override the bot at any time?

Yes, at any second. If a technician or receptionist types in WhatsApp, the bot pauses automatically and hands the conversation over — the client never notices a seam.

We sync directly via API. Any slot booked on WhatsApp updates your existing Acuity or Google Calendar — and whether the Acuity link stays or goes is your choice.
