Every question answered. Plainly.

No marketing fluff. Here is exactly how the WhatsApp concierge integrates into Lashdou's daily salon operations.

Average onboarding · 5–7 business days

01Will a WhatsApp bot feel cold or robotic to our boutique lash clients?

Not at all. The conversational tone is crafted specifically to match Lashdou's warm, calm, boutique aesthetic. Short, polite sentences. The salon's own words for its services. No corporate phrasing, no emoji spam.

“Warm porcelain and quiet precision — never generic corporate automated phrasing.”
02Can our salon staff override the bot if a client has a unique request?

Yes — at any second. If a technician or receptionist types in WhatsApp, the bot pauses automatically and hands the conversation over. Staff keep full control; the bot is a first responder, not a gatekeeper.

03What happens to our current Acuity booking link?

We sync directly via API. Any slot booked on WhatsApp updates your existing Acuity or Google Calendar, so nothing is double-booked. The Acuity link can stay as a fallback or be retired — your choice, either way.

04Who owns our client data and phone numbers?

Lashdou does — fully. Client data, consultation intake notes and telephone rosters remain the exclusive property of the salon. Tiyan Digital operates as an infrastructure orchestrator under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA): no data is sold, shared or used for any other client, and everything can be exported or deleted on request. See the Privacy Policy.

05How long does implementation take from start to finish?

Onboarding averages 5–7 business days for the Concierge tier — tone mapping, calendar connection, service triage and staff onboarding included. Launch is typically faster (about 5 business days); Studio, with its extra routing and review flows, runs 10–14 business days. Timelines are estimates and confirmed on the walkthrough call.
