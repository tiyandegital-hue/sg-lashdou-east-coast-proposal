Behind the scenes: what the bot does for Lashdou

While clients experience effortless conversation on WhatsApp, the engine runs the synchronisation underneath.

01

Instant slot locking

Direct synchronisation with your existing Google Calendar or Acuity diary. Once a client selects a slot, it is held — no double-bookings, no manual diary checks.

02

Total staff override

Front-desk staff can jump into any WhatsApp conversation from WhatsApp Web or their phone. The bot yields instantly and hands over gracefully — a human co-pilot, not a wall.

03

24-hour & day-of reminders

Replaces uncomfortable manual phone calls. The bot sends courteous, East Coast-toned reminders, and if a client cancels, it offers the slot to the waitlist right away.

04

Bespoke aftercare

Automatically delivers authentic YUMI Keratin aftercare instructions after treatment, and follows up when the next appointment would naturally be due.
