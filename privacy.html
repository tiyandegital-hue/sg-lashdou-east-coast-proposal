Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Lashdou WhatsApp booking concierge proposal website, built by Tiyan Digital (founder Tanjir) for Lashdou Salon, 66 East Coast Road, Singapore. It is written in plain language, as required by Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

What this site is

A proposal and working demo. It shows how a WhatsApp booking concierge would work for Lashdou. The chat conversations on this site are scripted samples — no real client data is shown, and the demo does not connect to Lashdou's real calendar or WhatsApp account.

What data we collect

  • Walkthrough form (FAQ page): your name, WhatsApp phone number, preferred contact time and service of interest — only what is needed to arrange a 20-minute walkthrough. You give explicit consent via the checkbox before submitting.
  • Demo interactions: taps inside the scripted chat simulator stay in your browser. Nothing you type or tap in the demo is sent to a server.
  • Essential cookies: one local preference (whether you acknowledged the cookie notice). No tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no analytics on this proposal site.

How the data is used

Form details are used only to arrange and conduct the walkthrough you requested — a reply on WhatsApp or email about this proposal, and nothing else. We do not sell, rent or share your details with any third party, and we do not add you to any marketing list.

WhatsApp compliance

The concierge described on this site would message clients only on WhatsApp Business, only about their bookings, reminders and aftercare, and only where the client has consented or the message is a booking they requested. Clients can opt out at any time by replying STOP or asking staff to stop messages. No promotional broadcasts are sent without explicit opt-in.

Data ownership

Lashdou's client data — customer records, consultation notes and telephone rosters — remains the exclusive property of Lashdou. Tiyan Digital acts only as an infrastructure orchestrator: it builds and maintains the system, it does not own the data. On request, all data can be exported or deleted.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). Google may receive your IP address and browser information when fonts load, under Google's own privacy policy. No other third-party trackers, maps or analytics are embedded.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact Tiyan Digital via the walkthrough form on the FAQ page, or Lashdou directly at ask@lashdou.com or +65 8979 6656.

Changes

If this proposal becomes a live system, this policy will be updated to describe the live data flows (calendar sync, WhatsApp Business messaging, reminder scheduling) before launch.
